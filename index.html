What we craft

Four disciplines, one workshop. Every order is measured, cut and finished under the same roof.

School Uniforms

Uniforms built for the school day

Full uniform ranges cut to your school's exact colours and specification — blazers, tunics, trousers, skirts and PE kit sized for growing learners and finished to survive a full year of break times.

Custom Embroidery

Crests, badges and names, stitched not stuck

School crests, house badges and individual name tags embroidered directly into the fabric, so they hold their shape and colour through every wash for years, not weeks.

Precision Printing

Sharp, durable prints on fabric

Sports kits, event t-shirts and promotional wear printed with clean, exact registration no cracking, fading or peeling after repeated wear and wash.

Tailored Sewing

Alterations and made-to-measure finishing

Hemming, resizing, repairs and made-to-measure garments for staff and learners who need a fit that off-the-rack sizing can't give them.
